Digital literature

In my works of digital literature, code, animation, voice and music transform the way poetry is read and explored. The texts are in Spanish.

Works

Alfonsina: Trap y poesía

2026. My role: concept, curation, AI prompts, musicalization, videos and website. Poems by Alfonsina Storni.

A tribute to Alfonsina Storni: eight poems from El dulce daño (1918), unchanged word for word, recontextualized within trap, reggaeton and dembow. A transmedia remediation conceived as a digital album-book and accompanied by a manifesto on artificial intelligence in artistic creation.

Bolero techno

2025–2026. My role: concept, AI prompts, musicalization, videos and website. Poems by Abraham Truxillo.

A work of digital literature that turns twenty-seven poems from the book Bolero techno by Abraham Truxillo (Manofalsa, Lima, 2025) into songs through artificial intelligence. It starts from a question: what happens to a poem when it is heard instead of read?

Listen to the workCode on GitHub

Bestiario marino

2021. My role: project design, interaction design, programming, animation and voice. Texts and visual poems by Abraham Truxillo.

A work of electronic literature that combines animated visual poetry, sound and short fiction.

See the workCode on GitHub

  • Discussed in an article in the Bulletin of Hispanic Studies (Liverpool University Press, 2026).
  • Honorable mention, Julio Torri National Prize for Short Fiction and Digital Literature 2020–2021, Coahuila Ministry of Culture and Centro de Cultura Digital (results in Zócalo).
  • Included in the Lit(e)lat Anthology, volume 2.0, of the Latin American Electronic Literature Network (2025).
  • Shown at The Wrong Biennale 2023/24, VerbivocoVirtual pavilion.
