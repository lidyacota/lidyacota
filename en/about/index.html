About me

I’m a computer scientist trained at Mexico’s National Polytechnic Institute (IPN). I did graduate studies toward a Master of Science in Computer Science at the IPN’s Center for Computing Research, where I focused on knowledge representation and natural semantic networks.

For six years I led the innovation area of a software development company. I set the technical approach for each solution and coordinated the development teams; together we built data analysis, machine learning and artificial intelligence prototypes for clients in the United States and Mexico.

Before that, starting in 2002, I worked in technical support, on Unix servers and in SAP programming for finance and tax.

Since 2019 I’ve been developing my own digital projects. I built and published an iOS app, and I designed and have run the entire digital business of an online literature school: product, advertising, websites, analytics and student support.

I am also a digital literature artist: my web works weave together poetry, code, animation, voice and music.
